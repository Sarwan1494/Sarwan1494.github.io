Overview

This board is a compact closed-loop driver for a NEMA17 stepper motor, built for a robotic arm project. It runs field-oriented control on an STM32F103, reading phase current through two INA181 amplifiers and rotor position from an external magnetic encoder, so the motor can be commanded smoothly instead of driven open loop.

  • DRV8844 H-bridge drives both motor phases directly from the 24V rail.
  • Per-phase current sensing feeds the FOC control loop running on the STM32.
  • CAN transceiver lets the board sit on a shared bus with other joints in a multi-axis arm.
  • On-board buck and LDO regulators generate the 5V and 3.3V logic rails from the 24V input.
  • ESD and TVS protection on the CAN and USB lines for robustness outside a bench setting.
  • USB Micro-B plus BOOT1/RESET1 buttons for firmware flashing without a separate programmer.
