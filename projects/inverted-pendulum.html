Overview

A Furuta pendulum, also called a rotary inverted pendulum, modelled in Onshape. I built the parts, combined them into an assembly with mates, and animated the motion.

  • A stand holds a 17HE12-1204S NEMA17 stepper motor, which turns the encoder mount.
  • The pendulum is a bent arm that pivots in the encoder mount and carries a mass at its tip.
  • An AS5600 magnetic encoder on the encoder mount reads the pendulum angle.
  • 8 part instances and 8 mates in the full assembly.
