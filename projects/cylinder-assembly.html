Cylinder Assembly

Onshape 3D CAD Assembly Mates

The barrel is shown transparent so the piston and its seals are visible through the full stroke.

Overview

A cylinder assembly modelled in Onshape. I built the parts, combined them into an assembly with mates, and animated the piston and rod through the full stroke.

  • Piston with multiple seal grooves, running in a cylindrical barrel.
  • Piston rod exiting through a rod-end cap on the mounting side.
  • Square mounting flange with four through holes.
  • Two ports on the barrel, one near each end cap.

Next steps

  • Parameterized stroke length. Drive the model from a stroke length parameter, so one model covers different strokes.
  • Mounting variants. Design additional variants with trunnion and rear end flange mounting.
  • Tie rod construction. Add a tie rod version, with the end caps held together by tie rods along the barrel.
